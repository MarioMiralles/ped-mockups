Case 1 · Primary focus

Dallas, Texas: PhalloFILL Wrongful-Death Lawsuit

Patient death: September 3, 2025
Cause No.DC-26-20517Varkony v. Moore et al.
Court116th DistrictDallas County, Texas
Petition filedSep 28, 2026First Amended Original Petition
Treatment records4 sessionsDated Jun 25 – Sep 3, 2025, per the Petition

A wrongful-death lawsuit filed in Dallas County, Texas, raises serious concerns about the administration of hyaluronic acid dermal fillers for penile girth enhancement.

According to the First Amended Original Petition filed September 28, 2026, Iris Varkony, the mother of the deceased patient identified as O.V., brought legal claims against William Moore, AdvancedYOU, The PhalloFILL Clinics, and several additional defendants.

What does the lawsuit allege?

The Petition alleges that:

  1. A.William Moore founded and commercialized the PhalloFILL penile enhancement procedure.
  2. B.Moore was identified as the injector in the patient's treatment records.
  3. C.Moore lacked the medical licensing and qualifications necessary to perform, direct, or supervise the injections.
  4. D.The patient did not receive appropriate medical evaluation, supervision, or disclosure of certain risks.
  5. E.The patient underwent a further PhalloFILL injection session on September 3, 2025, and died later that day.
Reported autopsy findings
Hyaluronic acid pulmonary embolism

The Petition states that an autopsy identified hyaluronic acid pulmonary embolism as the cause of death. It further alleges that pathological findings were consistent with embolized filler material associated with multiple treatment sessions.

These statements are drawn from the plaintiff's court filing. The underlying autopsy report has not been independently evaluated for this article.

Case 2

Bangkok, Thailand

Reported death of Igho "Tiny" Ubiribo

Reported death: March 2026

Media reports described the death of Igho "Tiny" Ubiribo, a 43-year-old Nigerian-British businessman and social media personality, following a reported penile enhancement procedure in Bangkok, Thailand.

According to those reports, the procedure involved injections of approximately 40 mL of hyaluronic acid filler with lidocaine.

The reports stated that Ubiribo subsequently experienced a medical emergency and that pulmonary embolism was identified as a possible or reported cause of death.

Why this case mattersThe reported circumstances highlight the importance of understanding potential complications of injectable materials, the volume administered, and the availability of appropriate medical evaluation and emergency care.

The circumstances and precise medical cause of death have not been independently confirmed. A temporal association with a procedure does not, by itself, establish causation.

Case 3

Paris, France

Reported death of Ehud Arye Laniado

Reported death: March 2019

Ehud Arye Laniado, a Belgian-Israeli diamond trader, was reported to have died while undergoing a penile enlargement procedure in Paris, France.

Media accounts described a cardiac event during the procedure, which reportedly involved an injection.

Why this case mattersThis case illustrates why even elective cosmetic procedures require appropriate medical assessment, informed consent, qualified providers, and readiness to respond to medical emergencies.

Public reporting does not establish that an injected material caused the reported cardiac event.

Potential risks

Understanding the Potential Risks of Penile Enhancement Injections

Penile enhancement injections are medical procedures and may carry significant risks. Depending on the material, injection technique, patient characteristics, and other factors, potential complications may include:

  • 01Infection and inflammatory reactions.
  • 02Nodules, irregularities, or unwanted changes in appearance.
  • 03Tissue injury or necrosis.
  • 04Vascular injury or occlusion.
  • 05 · Can be life-threateningEmbolic complications, which can be life-threatening.
  • 06Allergic reactions or other serious medical emergencies.

Not every complication is caused by the same mechanism, and risks differ between products and techniques.

Before treatment

Questions Every Patient Should Ask

Before undergoing a penile enhancement procedure, patients should consider asking:

  1. Who will personally perform my procedure, and what medical license and training do they hold?
  2. What exact product or substance will be injected?
  3. Is the product FDA-approved for this specific use, or is the proposed use off-label?
  4. What complications are possible, including rare but serious complications?
  5. What medical evaluation will I receive beforehand?
  6. What emergency procedures and resources are available if a complication occurs?
  7. What follow-up care will be provided?

Patients should be given the opportunity to discuss the benefits, limitations, alternatives, and risks of a proposed treatment before consenting.
